what is in a memento?

A favor your guests actually play with

Forget the favors that get left on the table. Our vending machine turns a simple gift into a little moment of joy. Guests hear the clink of the token, twist the knob and get a surprise to take home.

  • 🪙
    Interactive funGuests of every age line up to try it. It's an icebreaker, an activity and a favor all in one, and we provide the tokens.
  • 🎨
    Personalized designsStickers, temporary tattoos and/or mini prints from our design library, your own art, or something we draw just for you. The machine display can show your names, date or a punny message too.
  • 🚚
