Frequently asked

Can't find what you're looking for? Please send us a note.

What kinds of events do you do?

Weddings, birthday parties, bridal showers, baby showers, corporate events and social gatherings. If there are people who'd enjoy a surprise, we'd love to be there!

What goes in the machine?

Stickers, temporary tattoos and mini prints, or a mix! The machine has three slots, so each one can hold something different.

Can we customize the designs?

Yes! Choose from our design library (customizing those is free), provide your own art for free, or request a custom design for $50 each.

How much does it cost?

Machine rental is $200 for 2 hours up to $425 for 5 hours, including delivery, setup and pickup. The art is $0.50–$2 per item depending on the type, quantity and size. Please see our pricing page for details.

Do guests need to bring coins?

Nope! We provide tokens for your guests.

Are the temporary tattoos safe for skin?

Yes, our temporary tattoos are skin-safe and come off with soap and water or a little baby oil.

Where are you located? Do you travel?

We're based in Capitol Hill, Seattle. There's no travel fee within 25 miles. It's $75 for 26–40 miles, $100 for 41–60 miles and $125 for 61–80 miles. Beyond 80 miles we'll quote you directly, and overnight accommodation may need to be covered.

Do you stay during the event?

Yes. We set up the machine, keep an eye on it during your event, and take it away afterward.

How do we book?

Please fill out our interest form and we'll email you about availability within 1–5 business days. A 25% deposit secures your date.

What is your payment policy?

A minimum 25% deposit is due at booking to secure your date, and deposits are non-refundable. The remaining balance is due in full at least 14 days before your event.

Do you offer corporate pricing?

Yes! Please reach out through our booking inquiry for corporate pricing.
