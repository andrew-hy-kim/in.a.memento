Frequently asked

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a note.

What kinds of events do you do?

We're starting with weddings, and we love birthdays, baby and bridal showers, corporate events and community pop-ups too. If there are people who'd enjoy a surprise, we're in!

Where are you located? Do you travel?

We're based in Seattle, WA and serve the greater Seattle area. Traveling a little farther? Ask us — a travel fee may apply.

Do guests need to bring coins?

Nope! We provide tokens for your guests. You can hand them out with place cards, at the bar or leave them in a dish by the machine.

Can we customize the stickers and tattoos?

Yes — that's the best part. We design around your names, date, colors, theme, pets and inside jokes. You'll see proofs before anything is printed.

Are the temporary tattoos safe for skin?

Yes, our temporary tattoos are made to be skin-safe and come off with soap, water or a little baby oil.

How much space does the machine need?

Just a small table or flat surface, ideally indoors or under cover. We'll handle the set-up and the display sign.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend reaching out as soon as you have a date — ideally at least X weeks before your event so there's plenty of time for custom designs.

How much does it cost?

See our pricing page for packages, or reach out for a custom quote.
