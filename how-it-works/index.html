How to use the machine

  1. Grab a token. Tokens are handed out with place cards, at the bar, or from a little dish by the machine — your call.
  2. Pick a slot. Each slot can hold a different surprise: stickers, tattoos or a mix.
  3. Insert & twist. Drop the token in and turn the knob all the way around.
  4. Collect your memento! Open the little door and pull out your surprise mini print.

1 token = 1 surprise mini print ✨
