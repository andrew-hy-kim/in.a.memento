How it works

From inquiry to event day

1. Send an inquiry

Please fill out our interest form and we'll email you about availability within 2 business days.

2. Plan together

We'll talk through your event and designs, usually over a Google Meet call or by email.

3. Book your date

A 25% deposit holds your date. See pricing for details.

4. Event day

We set up the machine, keep an eye on it, and take it away when you're done.

Using the machine

Each slot can hold a different surprise: stickers, tattoos and/or mini prints.
