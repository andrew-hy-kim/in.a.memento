Everyone goes home with something they picked out themselves.

for your guests

How to use the machine

  1. Grab a token. We leave a dish of tokens by the machine, or you can hand them out with place cards.
  2. Pick a slot. Each of the three slots can hold a different surprise: stickers, tattoos and/or mini prints.
  3. Insert & twist. Drop the token in and turn the knob all the way around.
  4. Collect your memento! Open the little door and pull out your surprise memento.

1 token = 1 surprise memento ✨
